# Writing topic queue for drqaria.njmcmedicsupp.com

The writer takes the **first topic whose slug has no built page** in `drqaria/`.
A used topic has a page, so it is skipped automatically on the next run. Nothing
needs editing to advance the queue.

Refilling this list is the recurring human task. When fewer than four unused
topics remain, add more.

## What belongs here

This is Dr. Qaria's personal site, not a company blog. A topic earns a place
only if **he is one of the few people who could write it**: someone with a
doctorate in molecular microbiology and a doctorate in cancer biology who now
sources medicines for markets where the checking that happens is whatever the
buyer chooses to do.

The test for a topic: could a competent copywriter with no laboratory
experience and no import experience produce the same piece? If yes, it belongs
on the NJMC Insights blog instead, not here.

Every piece is written in the **first person**. It may draw on his own published
work, which is real and citable, and on what he has actually seen in trade. It
may not invent a regulatory figure, a threshold or a statistic. If a topic can
only be written well by quoting a number he cannot source, it is not ready.
