I asked WHO for permission to cite its data. It answered.

When I first wrote to them, I did not expect an answer.

Published 7 November 2026 1 min read Dr. Qaria

On 29 October 2026 the answer came, and it was yes. Thank you to the WHO Permissions Team for reading the request and answering it.

What did WHO allow PharmaTrust to do?

Good news: the World Health Organization has allowed PharmaTrust to cite selected numerical data, with attribution to the source. The source is The International Pharmacopoeia. This will help us serve low-income countries, where many buyers and laboratories have the least means of checking a certificate.

Source: The International Pharmacopoeia, Thirteenth Edition, 2025, World Health Organization.
